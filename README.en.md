# ⚔️ The Legend of Ignivar

[🇧🇷 Português](README.md) | 🇺🇸 English

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.8%2B-blue?logo=python&logoColor=white)

A small **medieval pixel-art CRPG** made in Python with pygame. Explore forests, mines and fortresses, fight goblins, orcs and skeletons, rescue dwarves and defeat **Ignivar, the Black Dragon**, in a 10-mission adventure that takes less than an hour to finish.

All graphics are generated by the code itself: there are no external image or sound files. The whole game fits in a single `.py` file.

> 🌐 **Note:** the in-game text is in Brazilian Portuguese.

## 📜 Story

Ignivar, the Black Dragon, has awakened and gathered goblins and orcs under his wings. Pedraforte (Stoneforge), the last kingdom of the dwarves, is in danger. Guided by **Borin Stonehammer**, guardian of the dwarves, you travel through forests, hills, mines, swamps and frozen peaks until you reach the dragon's lair.

## ✨ Features

- **3 playable classes:** Warrior, Mage and Archer, each with 4 unique abilities.
- **10 missions** with varied goals: defeat all enemies, rescue dwarves or beat a boss.
- **Turn-based combat** with spells, poisons, temporary buffs, critical hits, dodges and animations.
- **Procedurally generated maps** across 10 different settings, always with a guaranteed path to the exit.
- **Character progression:** levels, new abilities and weapon and armor upgrades.
- **Dwarven camp** between missions, with a shop and a place to rest.
- **4 bosses**, including a dragon that unleashes Black Fire Breath.
- **Pixel art made entirely in code**, with no external files.

## 🚀 How to play

### Requirements

- Python 3.8 or newer
- pygame 2

### Installation

```bash
git clone https://github.com/bcastroalves/lenda-de-ignivar.git
cd lenda-de-ignivar
pip install -r requirements.txt
python lenda_de_ignivar.py
```

> 💡 On Linux or macOS you may need to use `python3` and `pip3`.

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys / WASD | Move on the map and navigate menus |
| Enter / Space | Confirm |
| Esc | Go back / open the character sheet (on the map) |
| H | Drink a Health Potion (on the map) |
| M | Drink a Mana Potion (on the map) |

On the map, just walk into an enemy to start a fight. Nearby enemies will also chase you.

## ⚙️ Mechanics

- **Combat:** each turn you choose between Attack, Abilities, Items or Flee. You cannot flee from bosses.
- **Physical damage** uses Attack and is heavily reduced by the target's Defense. **Magic damage** uses Magic and ignores most of the Defense.
- **Poisons** deal damage for 3 turns. **Buffs** such as War Cry also last 3 turns.
- **After each victory** you recover part of your health and mana. Mana also regenerates a little every turn.
- **Chests** hidden on the maps contain gold and potions.
- **Defeat:** you can restart the current mission with your character as it was when the mission began.

## 💡 Tips

- Save potions for boss fights.
- Against groups, use abilities that hit every enemy (Whirlwind, Blizzard, Arrow Rain).
- Take out Goblin Shamans first, because they heal their allies.
- Before facing the dragon, keep your health high on the turn he is about to use Black Fire Breath.

## 🛠️ Customization

All game data lives in dictionaries at the top of the file, so it is easy to tweak the difficulty or add new content:

| Dictionary | What it controls |
|---|---|
| `CLASSES` | Starting stats, per-level growth and abilities of each class |
| `SKILLS` | Cost, damage, effects and required level of the abilities |
| `INIMIGOS` | Enemy health, attack, defense, rewards and behavior |
| `MISSOES` | Name, setting, goal, enemies and text of each mission |
| `THEMES` | Colors and terrain type of each setting |
| `SPRITES` | Character pixel art as 16×16 character grids |

Sprites are drawn as text: each letter stands for a color in the `PAL` palette and a dot (`.`) is transparent. To create a new character, just draw a new grid.
